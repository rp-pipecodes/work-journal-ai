# Work Summary combines a selected period with current commitments

Issue #237 replaces the AI-written Standup Post with a personal Work Summary: all Notes in its selected date range, ordinary Tasks and recurring Task Occurrences completed in that range, plus all currently Open Tasks, including future and Unscheduled commitments. The summary separates accomplishments in that period from commitments open now, because current Task state cannot establish what was open during a historical period. It may synthesize accomplishments and qualify inferred connections, but does not offer unsolicited priorities or next steps.

Work Summary remains a Main Window section reached from both the sidebar and Tray Menu, under that name. It owns a date-range control like History's, independently of History, and has no Project filter: its purpose spans all Notes and Tasks, and Tasks have no Project. This revisits ADR 0034's rejection of a separate range for a review surface: Work Summary now has a distinct purpose and includes current commitments, while History continues to own its own Filter.

This replaces the yesterday-only purpose of Standup Post while retaining [ADR 0027](0027-the-standup-post-never-replaces-yesterdays-digest.md)'s independent lossless clipboard path. [ADR 0034](0034-review-material-is-a-third-lossless-rendering.md)'s Review Material remains retrospective: including current commitments in Work Summary does not redefine that existing output.

Work Summary Material replaces Standup Material with the complete selected-period Notes and completions followed by all current Open Tasks, independently copyable without a model. This supersedes [ADR 0031](0031-standup-material-is-a-second-lossless-rendering.md)'s yesterday-shaped input while preserving its separation of lossless material from fallible prose; Yesterday's Digest and Review Material remain unchanged. Generated summaries remain temporary and non-authoritative: their original range and generation time remain visible, changes mark them outdated, and regeneration is explicit.
