# Recurring Tasks have one Open occurrence

A Recurring Task follows a fixed civil-time calendar cadence and has exactly one Open Task Occurrence at a time. A weekly cadence may select multiple weekdays—the single Open occurrence advances through them in order rather than materializing simultaneous Tasks. Monday-based weeks are counted from the week containing the starting date, and selected weekdays before that date are ignored; editing the start, selected days, or cadence immediately reanchors the series and replaces the current Open occurrence without claiming it was completed. A series created from a past start opens on its latest elapsed slot; an overdue occurrence remains open until completed, when it is retained in the Task's expandable history and the Task advances to the next future slot, skipping missed slots rather than generating a backlog. The cadence may be daily, weekly, monthly, yearly, or every N such units, follows the user's local time across timezone changes, and continues until explicitly stopped; edits affect the continuing series because there are no per-occurrence exceptions. Monthly and yearly rules retain their intended day through shorter periods rather than drifting after a fallback date. A mistaken completion can be undone only for the latest occurrence while its successor remains Open and unchanged, atomically removing that successor so the one-Open invariant survives without discarding later decisions. Rejected: cloning every occurrence into an independent Task, which can flood the list after time away, and calculating the next slot from completion time, which makes a fixed commitment such as “every Monday at 14:00” drift.
